The platform

OpenChoreo is WSO2's open-source developer platform for Kubernetes. Teams describe their services, and the platform handles building, deploying and running them.

My project · Progressive delivery

I'm building progressive delivery into OpenChoreo. A new version gets a small slice of real traffic first. The platform watches its health and widens the rollout only while it holds up. If it starts failing, traffic goes back to the stable version before most users notice.

What it is teaching me

How to ship inside a large open-source codebase: design reviews, tests that earn their keep, and release discipline.
