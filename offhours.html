SEDS Ruhuna
Observation Division

Running the night shift

On most clear nights I’m out stargazing with my own telescope. I also manage the Observation Division at SEDS Ruhuna. We run the "Ad Astra" night observation camps and community stargazing meetups, look after the chapter's telescopes and mounts, and teach members to observe and photograph the sky.

It started at school: seven years in the Astronomical and Space Science Society at Ranabima Royal College.
