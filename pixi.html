A little companion for your site.

Pixi is the dot-matrix face in the corner of this page. It blinks, wanders, naps and plays hide and seek, and it can be picked up and thrown. Every motion runs on springs.

What it does

Small, alive, out of the way

Feels physical

Drag it, throw it, flick it off the bottom edge to tuck it away. Velocity carries through, edges rubber-band, landings bounce.

Has a personality

Moods, heart eyes, sneezes, dizzy spells, dot art and a dozen hidden secrets. It speaks rarely, so it never nags.

Plays nicely

Respects reduced motion, works with touch and keyboard, adapts to light and dark, and steps aside for your menus.

Install

One line

Paste this before </body>.

<script src="https://prageethbanuka.github.io/assets/pixi-embed.js" defer></script>

Give it your own words (optional):

<script>
  window.pixiConfig = {
    name: 'Pixi',
    hello: 'Hi! Welcome in.',
    lines: ['Try the search.', 'New post every Friday.'],
    sound: false
  };
</script>
